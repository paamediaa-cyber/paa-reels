## Filming tips
- Film from the **passenger seat**, never the driver. Phone on a mount, 4K 60fps, stabilisation on.
- Golden hour (sunrise / ~5:30pm) for the best colour.
- Record 2–3 passes per board so you have options.
- Shot 4: film from a safe, legal roadside spot.

## Audio
- Trending upbeat/electronic sound with a **beat drop at 4s** (when the car passes the board).
- Optional: add a light "whoosh" SFX on the pass.
